# Design Direction

Confirmed principles only:

- The human owns page composition, typography, colors, animation concepts, interaction concepts, copy, visual references, and final UI details.
- The project should stay strange, theatrical, comedic, cinematic, and over-serious about an unnecessary subject.
- The blue versus red quote is central to the emotional premise.
- The current foundation should remain easy to redesign.
- Avoid generic AI website polish unless specifically requested.
- Avoid SaaS layouts, dashboards, hero-section marketing patterns, excessive rounded cards, glassmorphism, arbitrary gradients, generic feature grids, generic footers, and generic CTA sections.
- Do not add visual references, palettes, motion language, or detailed art direction without human direction.

Open design decisions:

- Final page composition
- Typography
- Color system
- Animation timing
- Character imagery
- Sound direction
- Card flip behavior
- Timeline breach visual language
- Vote and results presentation
