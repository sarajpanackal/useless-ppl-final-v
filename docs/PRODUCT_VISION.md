# Product Vision

USELESS PEOPLE is an interactive fictional experience about side characters, NPC-like characters, comic-relief characters, overlooked characters, and story people who never received protagonist-level narrative care.

The conceptual thesis is:

"You can be the prettiest shade of blue, but if their favorite color is red, it does not matter."

The site takes characters who were metaphorically the blue and gives them another timeline where canon briefly stops ignoring them.

The intended experience can begin absurd and comedic, then unexpectedly become cinematic, dramatic, emotional, or strangely sincere. The point is not usefulness. The point is unnecessary narrative seriousness applied to people who were denied it.

This should never become a practical productivity tool, recommendation system, social platform, AI assistant, education product, or conventional application. Its technical quality should support the useless premise, not replace it.
